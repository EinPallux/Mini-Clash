## [Unreleased] — v0.2.0 "Shatterbridge" (in progress)

### Added
- **Offline play (airplane mode):** a service worker precaches the entire build at install — app shell, hashed bundles, game assets, icons, fonts (the list is injected by a Vite build step, so caching never depends on which first-visit requests raced past worker activation). After the first visit the full game — hub, champion select, Bridge Brawl vs bots — boots and plays with no network. Verified in CI by the bridge smoke's offline-reload leg.
- **Acceptance harness upgrades:** `scripts/botmatch.mjs` now reports per-tick p95 (~0.4 ms vs the 4 ms budget) and `scripts/smoke-bridge.mjs` runs the full acceptance path in CI: select (reroll + bench swap) → rigged win (`?rig=win`) → slab → podium → summary → history → offline reload.
- **Bridge Brawl simulation (full match loop, sim-side):** The Shatterbridge map def (deck, rails, brush pockets, orb pads, gates, lane), Watchtowers (Mini-first aggro, instant switch onto champion-attackers, +40% ramp, backdoor protection, destroy-in-order invulnerability, nav stamps that open when a tower falls), Clash Core (defense pulse, exposure after both towers, destruction → victory freeze + `matchOver`), Mini waves every 25 s (Bruiser/Zapper/Ram with per-kind AI: lane marching over A*, aggro/leash, zapper missiles, ram siege priority, +3%/min scaling), spawn-barrier walls that drop at 0:20, health orbs on the pads (heal + energy + XP with ally splash), brush concealment (hidden enemies are omitted from snapshots — map-hack impossible; attack/cast reveals for 1.5 s).
- **Economy:** starting 500 g, ambient XP/gold (half while dead), Mini bounties (gold split / XP full within 10 u), champion kill credit (killing blow or latest damager, 10 s assist window, streak bounties capped +300, assist pool split, takedown XP split), tower bounties (150 g global + 90 XP), levels 1–10 from the XP curve with +4% heal level-up moments, R hard-gated until level 4, death timers `5 + 1.5×level` (cap 20 s), fountain-plate fast heal.
- **Items live in the sim:** stat aggregation into champion totals (incl. haste-scaled cooldowns and the 2.5 attacks/s cap), shield absorb pools, and all 12 passive hooks (windrunner, executioner, stormweaver, juggernaut, nullwave, lifesteal, dragonfang, starcore burn, titan, phantom, lifebloom, overclock stub) + the 4 relic actives (Blink Prism, Purge Bell, Ember Flask, Horn of Rally). Shop honors the fountain/death windows and component upgrades (T2/T3 consume + discount their base item).
- **Bot AI (in-sim, deterministic, honest):** Recruit/Veteran/Elite tiers with per-tier reaction cadence (0.4 s → 0.2 s), skillshot aim noise + velocity lead, retreat thresholds with hysteresis, orb rotations, wave-front push discipline, structure-siege commits, elite skillshot dodging/kiting/focus-fire/ult-holding, relic usage, per-champion build orders, and Aggro/Guardian/Objective/Chaotic personalities. Bots read the same brush-concealed state as players and issue ordinary intents.
- **Balance harness:** `scripts/botmatch.mjs` runs seeded headless bot matches in bulk and reports lengths, winners, team winrates and champion K/D/A.
- **Champions +4 (sim + data + Training Grounds visuals):** **Mortis** (Soul Ledger inscriptions with attack refunds, Soulbolt that carries through Mini kills, telegraphed Grasping Stacks root — longer vs inscribed, Overdue Maelstrom moving channel), **Rattle** (Loose Bones dash charges, Fan of Knives, Rattle Step dash + destructible skull return-blink, Marrow Harvest blink-strike with kill-refund watch), **Grukk** (Toll Paid damage stacks, Skewer tip-pull lunge, War Bellow that fears Minis + self-shield, three-slam Seismic Tantrum via multi-charge recast), **Sylva** (Pollen Trail walking garden, blooming Thorn Dart, Blooming Ward heal/cleanse/dampen zone, Wildwood Embrace roots extended per bloomed flower). New generic sim capabilities backing them: delayed/telegraphed areas, caster-following channels, dash sweeps with tip-pull, destructible markers + return blinks, blink-strikes, self-shields, fear (Minis scatter), damage-amp buffs, flower/zone entities, projectile fans and kill-piercing, multi-charge recasts — all data-driven opcodes with zod schemas. Per-kit Vitest coverage in the Training Grounds; FX timelines + synth cues authored for all four kits and the bridge structures/relics.
- **Protocol v0.2:** shop/relic intents, Mini/Tower/Core/Orb snapshots, match-state snapshot (clock, barrier, team kills, towers, next orb, victory), kill/towerDown/matchOver/purchase/barrierDown events; snapshots now carry gold/K/D/A/items/relic/shield/brush state per champion.
- **Bridge Brawl is playable from the hub (client):** the mode card launches a full 4v4 vs Veteran bots (1 human + 7, champion pool dealt without duplicates). The Shatterbridge renders as a real sky-bridge — deck-clipped fractured floor with frayed rail edges, continuous low rails, underdeck pillars, drifting rock islets and trees in the void, brush pockets (bushes + grass), orb pads, team-flagged gate lines, fountain plates with glow discs and banners, and battle wreckage. New battle actors: Watchtowers (Castle hexagon stack + ballista that aims at its target, team-flat roof, ramp glow, aggro tether — bright red when it targets *you*, invulnerability dome, roof blown off under 66% HP, lean + chimney smoke under 33%, full collapse into persistent rubble), Clash Core (plinth + floating team crystal, halo, shield dome until exposure, implosion on destruction), Minis (Blocky bodies with team tints, walk/attack/die clips, spear-armed Bruisers, shooting Zappers, Rams as rolling Castle siege engines, per-kind death FX incl. a collapsing ram wreck), health orbs (bobbing glow), Sylva's flowers and ward zone, Rattle's hovering skull marker (kegs are now data-driven bodies). Spawn barrier walls render as animated team-tinted energy fields that dissolve at 0:20; Overtime/Sudden Death shift the fog toward ember. Brush-hidden allies fade translucent (enemies are omitted server-side). Champion/mini/structure health bars are team-colored; right-click targeting picks enemy units only.
- **Champion Select v1 — "the Deal":** entering Bridge Brawl now runs the select ceremony — staggered card-flip deal of your champion and the three ally picks (bot names on the cards), two rerolls with a burst animation, rerolled champions slide to a team bench and swap back with a tap, hidden enemy silhouettes, a resolved-numbers passive tip for the held champion, and a 45 s timer ring around LOCK IN that auto-locks on timeout. The locked 8-seat lineup is exactly the roster the match simulates, and the loading veil shows your team's cards while the bridge loads.
- **Bridge HUD complete (UI_UX §8/§10–§12 for the v0.2 feature set):** match strip (team kills, tower pips, clock, gate countdown, orb timer) + OVERTIME/SUDDEN DEATH banner; team frames for both sides (portrait chips, levels, HP bars, respawn counts, brush-hidden enemies dim to "?"); killfeed (champion-chip kill cards, tower falls, surrender); lane-strip minimap (structures, champions, minis, orbs, self ring); gold + 6 item slots + relic slot with cooldown; **death screen = the shop** (T1/T2/T3 columns + relic row with per-item icons, component discounts shown, owned/affordable states, one-click buy with hammer-clink, sell row, denial toasts); hold-**Tab scoreboard** (both teams: level, K/D/A, items, gold, hidden markers, structure tallies); **pings** — hold-G radial wheel (Danger / Attack / Help / On-my-way) + Alt+right-click quick ping, world pulse rings and per-kind cues, **and sim-side bot reactions** (Danger forces nearby allies to disengage; rally pings outrank the core march for ~4.5 s); FTUE coach-marks (move → abilities → ping → shop-on-death, shown once); **surrender** in the Esc menu from 8:00 (solo-vs-bots concedes immediately, vote UI arrives with multiplayer); and the **end-of-match sequence** — VICTORY/DEFEAT slab → podium (MVP + runners-up with K/D/A) → match summary with **Play Again** re-queue and local match history (last 30, in localStorage). Ability icons for all six champions plus 22 distinct item/relic icons (game-icons.net, credited). The death-screen damage recap ships with damage attribution in v0.3 (noted in UI_UX §10).
- **Fixes that fell out of making the bridge playable:** clicks past the deck rails now clamp to the nearest walkable cell (the void is nav-stamped; orders used to dead-end silently), champion/keg materials are cloned per instance (hit-flashes and fades no longer leak across duplicate champions), prop sockets prefer dedicated weapon slots over bare hand bones, glow sprites use a soft radial texture instead of rendering as squares, and the Enter keypress that submits the guest-name screen no longer instantly launches a match from the hub.

### Fixed
- **Post-lane-phase stalemates:** rigged-structure smoke runs exposed that once towers fall, bot armies trade at mid indefinitely until Overtime (matches still end inside the sudden-death bound — the 12–18 min target gets a dedicated balance pass). Smoke tests now use `?rig=win` (paper enemy structures + idle enemy seats) to exercise the win sequence deterministically.
- **KayKit champions (Mortis, Rattle) rendered as collapsed blobs.** Two root causes: the asset pipeline measured bounding boxes *after* `quantize()` (which rewrites positions into normalized grid space), so the manifest bbox inflated and the client's normalization scale collapsed the model; and prop-socket lookup missed GLTFLoader-sanitized node names (`handslot.r` → `handslotr`), so weapons fell back to the model root. Bounds are now measured pre-transform, socket lookup checks candidates in priority order (dedicated weapon slots before bare hand bones) across both rig families, and the two KayKit rigs carry visual-scale compensation (1.35× / 1.3×) for the T-pose bbox headroom of their hat/hood.
- **Deployments 404'd on `game-assets/manifest.json`** (e.g. Vercel with the Vite preset): hosts that ran the client's bare `vite build` shipped without the pipeline-generated assets. Added a repo-root `vercel.json` (install/build/output wired to the root `pnpm build`), a Vite build guard that fails with an actionable message when `public/game-assets/` is missing, and a README **Build & deploy** section.
- **Vercel builds now succeed under any project configuration:** the client's `build` script runs the asset pipeline itself (`pnpm -w assets:build && vite build`) and a second `vercel.json` inside `packages/client` covers projects whose Root Directory points there — no dashboard setting can skip the pipeline anymore. The pipeline also launches through an esbuild bundler (`run.mjs`) instead of Node's version-dependent TypeScript stripping (`--experimental-strip-types` is 22.6+-only and had hard-failed the build), with the repo root located by workspace-marker walk-up so the bundle runs from anywhere.

### Balance
- **v0.2 balance pass (7 harness-driven rounds, ~250 seeded matches):** Match pacing — Mini scaling 3 → 4.5%/min, Core pulse 120 → 95, tower backdoor protection 50% → 35%, Overtime moved 16:00 → 15:00 with Corebreaker upgraded (core damage ×3, 5 Rams per wave), Sudden Death moved 20:00 → 17:30 with 1.5%/s decay. Result: every harness match finishes, lengths 8–18.6 min (avg ≈ 18 mixed, 17.3 Elite). Champions — **Fathom** reined in (HP 640→615, AD 62→58, AS 0.88→0.80, MS 3.6→3.45, range 6.5→6.0, Skipshot 90→70 base, Powder Rounds 30→20); **Rattle** toughened (HP 660→740, armor 24→28); **Grukk** sharpened (AD 63→67, War Bellow shield 164→190 base); **Mortis** empowered (HP 600→650, Grasping Stacks 95→112 + root 1.2→1.4 s, Maelstrom tick 70→82); **Sylva** bolstered (HP 620→680, Ward heal 30→40/s, Wildwood Embrace 120→150). All numbers mirrored into docs/CHAMPIONS.md and GAME_DESIGN. Residual bot-pool spread (Fathom hot, Sylva cold vs mixed tiers) tracks bot micro and is scheduled with the v0.3 bot-AI pass — noted in ROADMAP.
- Watchtower HP 3000 → 2400 and Clash Core 4200 → 3600; backdoor-protection radius 8 u → 11 u; Rams every 3rd wave → every 2nd; Bruisers/Zappers deal 2.5× to enemy Minis. First harness runs showed doc-spec sieges stalling matches past 25 min (waves stockpiled, structures outlived every push window); numbers mirrored into GAME_DESIGN §13.
- Champion takedown XP (60 × victim level) is split among killer + assisters (was effectively full-to-each in early implementation — blob kills snowballed levels).
- New: 500 g starting gold and fountain-plate regen (9% max HP + 15 Energy/s) — documented in GAME_DESIGN §12.

## [Unreleased] — v0.1.0 "Training Grounds" (in progress)

### Changed
- **UI direction locked (binding for all future screens):** hero-shooter menu language — sharp corners, skewX(−10°) parallelogram tabs/ribbons, italic-skewed Oswald display type + Barlow Semi Condensed UI text, flat panels with hairline borders and gold top edges, dark chrome nav bar, light diamond-pattern and dark streaked backdrops, gold selection accent, keycap hints, CSS-mask iconography (no emoji). Hub rebuilt as nav-bar + hero cards + mode tiles; HUD, settings, boot/name, Esc menu and death veil restyled to match. Spec lives in ART_DIRECTION §8.

### Added
- **Monorepo & tooling:** pnpm workspaces (data/sim/protocol/client/tools), TypeScript strict, Biome, Vitest, package-boundary checker, GitHub Actions CI (check · test · build + headless visual smoke with screenshot artifacts).
- **`@mini-clash/data`:** typed content definitions with zod validation — Rook & Fathom kits, dummy units, Training Grounds map, FX timelines, procedural SFX recipes, UI strings.
- **`@mini-clash/sim`:** deterministic 30 Hz simulation — PCG32 seeded RNG, navgrid A* with string-pulling and dynamic wall stamping, declarative ability-action interpreter, auto-attacks with homing missiles, champion passives, powder kegs, volleys, leaps/knock-ups, target dummies with DPS windows, trainer commands, state hashing for replay tests.
- **`@mini-clash/protocol`:** intent/snapshot/event contract shared by the worker link (and the v0.3 server later).
- **Asset pipeline:** manifest-driven gltf-transform optimization of the CC0 packs (23 shipped assets, 1.4 MiB total incl. skybox), per-asset metadata (bounds, clips, skeletons), budget enforcement.
- **Client:** boot → guest name → hub flow, settings (video/audio/controls/accessibility with live remapping), Three.js toon-ramp renderer with follow camera, shake and hit-stop, worker-hosted offline sim, snapshot interpolation, animated champion actors with prop sockets, instanced particle system, data-driven FX runner, WebAudio synth engine, in-world health bars/damage numbers/aim telegraphs, Training Grounds HUD (ability cooldown wipes, trainer panel, dummy DPS readouts, champion switcher, death/respawn flow, Esc menu).

### Fixed (during headless visual verification)
- zustand object-selector infinite re-render on the HUD; instanced floor vanishing to frustum culling; clicks using a frame-stale cursor; UnrealBloom NaN blackout (bloom removed, glow via additive sprites); black characters from missing normals on unlit exports; software-GL context loss (SwiftShader now clamps to the low quality profile); arena wall scale; shadow acne.
